## Learner errors
| Said | Better | Type |
|---|---|---|
| explain what I have **listened** · if you are **listening to** this wind | what I've **heard** · if you can **hear** the wind | listen vs hear (3×) |
| my job **is looking for** 30 tickets | my job **is to review** 30 tickets | be + to (4th time) |
| try **take this conclusion** | try **to draw** a conclusion | calque |
| otherwise I **can** capture everything | otherwise I **can't** capture everything | negation flipped |
| **the weather is raining** | **it's** raining | dummy it |
| read **loudly** · **in** the beginning of this year · between the client **with** the company | read **aloud** · **at** the beginning · between the client **and** the company | prepositions |
| respond **a** form · answer **to** questions | respond **to** a form · answer questions | verb patterns |
| a **house beach** · different **point of views** | a **beach house** · different **points of view** | noun compounds |
| I **was understanding** agencies | I **thought** he said "agencies" | state verb |
| what is the main **purpose** of these issues | what the main **cause** of these issues **is** | word choice + order |

## Good
- **Paused the meeting himself:** "Hold on, hold on. I got lost. Can I explain what I've heard so far?"
- Stayed in character: "Mike, can you please repeat what I need to do?"
- Reused: "I didn't **catch**…" · "just a **disclosure**" · "**misleading**" · "on **behalf** of".
- Took notes while listening. A reported question in the right order: "I was asking you if [city] is where the airport is located."

## Coach notes
- In customer support, **agents** are the human representatives (call-center staff); an **agency** is an organization. The teacher's "AI agent" reading added confusion.
- **Pre-listening context** helps: prior knowledge of the topic improves comprehension (the planning stage in Vandergrift & Tafaghodtari 2010). Use the teacher's idea: a one-line context, then natural speed.
- **Numbers:** THIR-ty (stress on the 1st syllable) vs thir-TEEN (2nd) vs three. **debit** /ˈdebɪt/ vs **debt** /det/.
