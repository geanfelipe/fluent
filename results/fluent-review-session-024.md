## Learner questions answered
- **sort out** = resolve or organize something messy. It's separable: *sort out the bug* / *sort the bug out* / *sort it out* (never *sort out it*). Compare: solve (logical problem), fix (broken). First try: "Not yet, but I'm going to sort it out tomorrow." ✅
- **Why the pronoun goes in the middle:** end-focus (Quirk et al., 1985). A pronoun is old information and unstressed, so the stressed particle goes last. Exception: inseparable verbs (*deal with it, look after it*).
