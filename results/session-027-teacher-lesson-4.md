## Good
- **Reported questions with past perfect** (C1 level): "he asked me if I **had understood**" · "I asked her if she **had given** it to her".
- Direct questions on demand: "**Should we** delay the deployment?" · "**Will you** review the code?"
- Self-corrected: married ~~with~~ **to** · explain ~~me~~ **to me** · ~~achieved~~ **reached** the shore · **knew** each other · it was getting dark.
- Applied Wednesday's corrections: "had surgery" · "we **spent** three hours".
- Retold a short story read without a translator, and told a ~15-minute personal story.
- Self-awareness: "If I stop to think a little bit, I can speak"; "My mind wants to avoid getting it wrong" (avoidance).
