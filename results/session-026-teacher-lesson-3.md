## Good
- **Second conditional:** "If I were the president, my priority would be primary education."
- "It's not different **from**…" — the reviewed form came out in free speech.
- Reused new vocabulary at once: "can **jeopardize** the work that we have completed so far."
- Self-corrected: "today deadline will not meet → **We will not meet the deadline**."
- Good questions: "Make or meet?", the exception for *at*.
- A structured opinion on education policy (problem → cause → proposal), with humor ("Houston, we have a problem").

## Coach notes
- **at night** is the fixed exception (*in* the morning / afternoon / evening, but ***at night***). For 10 PM, both *late in the evening* and *late at night* are fine.
- **meet the deadline** is the standard business phrase. *make the deadline* and *make it* (= arrive or finish in time) are also correct.
