## Listening drill: the key lesson
| The teacher said | The learner understood |
|---|---|
| Options: release the basic features on Friday **if** the instructions are ready, **or** delay everything until next Wednesday | "release some features on Friday, then finish the rest" (the condition was missed) |
| Action item: "contact support **today** and find out whether they can finish **by tomorrow afternoon**; if not, we delay and inform management" | "contact the support team and understand if they can finish **by Wednesday**" (wrong deadline) |

The learner got the gist but **missed the action item's deadline and condition**, which are the details that matter in real meetings.
**Rule for every update:** catch (1) the options, (2) the decision rule, (3) the action item: who / what / by when. Then confirm it: *"Just to confirm: I'll contact support today and check whether they can finish by tomorrow afternoon. If not, we delay and inform management."*
