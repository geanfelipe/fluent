## DONE → NEXT → BLOCKER template
- DONE: "So far, I've ..." (present perfect, concrete outputs)
- NEXT: "Next, I'm going to ... I expect to finish by ..."
- BLOCKER: "One open point: I need ... from ..." / "No blockers."
